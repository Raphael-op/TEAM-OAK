Product Requirements Document for UMIS Student Portal UI Redesign

Course: BU-209 Backend Engineering  
Project Team: TEAM-oak  
Author: Team Lead (Amojo Raphael\_25/0025)

1. Product Overview and Problem Statement

Current Problem: The current Babcock University student portal (OpenBaraza/UMIS) looks outdated and blocky. It has a plain grey and white background, heavy blocks of unformatted text inside notification panels, and a massive empty space of unused white space on the right side of the screen. Important school tasks like course registration steps and online exams are hidden inside text-heavy tables.

Proposed Solution: A modern and visually pleasing design update that brings in organized dashboard cards, an interactive step-by-step course registration guide, a cleaner navigation menu on the side, and a layout that fits nicely on any screen size.

2. Project Objectives

Modern Visual Appeal: Upgrade the look and feel using clean writing styles, organized card containers, soft drop shadows, and a professional color scheme.

Eliminate Wasted Space: Change the sparse single-column layout into an organized grid that uses screen space efficiently across computers, tablets, and phones.

Reduce Thinking Effort: Turn static text instructions like the five registration steps into interactive, visual progress indicators.

3. Target Users and Research Findings

Primary Users: Undergraduate and postgraduate students who log in every day to check grades, view fee statements, handle semester registration, and open online exams.

Secondary Users: University administrators who send out announcements and security notices.

Student Feedback Summary: Conversations with classmates show frustration with clicking through too many hidden menus, slow pages on mobile phone browsers, and confusion over which step of the course registration they are currently on.

4. Minimum Viable Product Features(MVP)

Redesigned Dashboard Grid: Quick summary cards showing your current grade point average, fee balances, active credits, and direct shortcut buttons for online exams.

Interactive Registration Stepper: A visual progress bar replacing the plain text five-step instructions for course selection, commencement, finance statement, select payments, and current charges.

Smart Notification Cards: Replacing plain tables with color-coded notification cards that separate urgent alerts from general announcements.

Collapsible Sidebar and Responsive Layout: Clean icon navigation and better adaptability for mobile devices.

5. User Stories

As a student, I want to see my current grade point average and fee balance right when I log in so that I do not have to search through hidden menus.

As a student, I want notifications to clearly highlight important deadlines using visual status badges.

As a student, I want a clean layout that works smoothly on my phone browser when I check my class schedule on the move.

6. User Interface Design Specifications

Color Palette: Deep navy and slate tones for structure and authority; bright blue or teal accents for clickable buttons; soft off-white background colors to reduce eye strain.

Typography: Clean and simple font styles with clear sizing differences for headings, body text, and small badges.

Component Styling: Raised white cards with rounded corners, subtle border lines, and soft drop shadows.

7. Success Metrics

Task Completion Speed: A measurable drop in the average time students spend completing their semester course registration.

Student Satisfaction: Positive feedback ratings about the visual look and ease of navigation in surveys taken after the update.

Support Inquiries: A decrease in helpdesk complaints regarding hard-to-find features or confusing registration steps.
